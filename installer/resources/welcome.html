GSR24M @VERSION@

Driver for the Allen & Heath GS-R24 FireWire card (and ZED-R16), Apple silicon Macs, macOS 13 or later.

Installs: the GSR24M application (Applications), the gsrd daemon (owns the card) and the GS-R24M FireWire audio plug-in.

When the installation ends, the Mac's sound stops for a few seconds (CoreAudio restarts).

This software never writes firmware to the card.
